# Summer Event: Padel & Barbecue

Last Tuesday, the Konato family gathered at the Meadow Club in Antwerp for one of those evenings that remind you why team culture matters. Padel courts, a smoking grill, and the kind of company that makes even Belgian weather irrelevant.

## On the court

We kicked things off with an hour of padel. Competitive spirit was at an all-time high — rallies were fierce, points were hard-fought, and everyone made very sure their colleagues knew exactly how good they were. By the end of the session, the only thing louder than the celebrations was the collective thirst for a cold drink. The refreshing beverages waiting on the sidelines came at exactly the right moment.

## Around the grill

Then came the barbecue. We would love to report clear skies and golden evening light — but this is Belgium, so you already know how that part of the story goes. What the weather failed to deliver, the Meadow Club more than made up for in atmosphere, food, and laughter. The accommodation was warm, the food was excellent, and the company was even better. *#mycolleaguesarebetterthanyours*

## Here's to many more

Evenings like this are a reminder that great work is built by great teams — and great teams need moments to simply enjoy each other's company away from the desk. A big thank you to everyone who organised the event, and to the whole Konato family for making it one to remember.

Here's to many more courts, more grills, and more memories ahead.
