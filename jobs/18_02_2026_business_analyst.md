# Business Analyst

![Business Analyst](jobs/images/ai_business_analyst.jpg)

At 9Yards, we design business and digital solutions that last. As a Business Analyst, you are the bridge between the business and IT — translating stakeholder needs into clear, actionable specifications that drive real transformation.

## What You'll Do

### Solution design

- Draft the "ideal" solution taking into account input from all stakeholders.
- Document and specify solutions using use cases, information modelling, and related techniques.
- Guard the scope, ensure quality, and follow up on planning and deadlines.

### Delivery and follow-up

- Collaborate closely with users, developers, architects, and project managers throughout the development process.
- Support and coordinate business acceptation tests to ensure delivered solutions meet requirements.

## Who You Are

- You have a first experience as a functional or business analyst.
- You have knowledge of UML, BPM, Agile, ICT, and related methodologies.
- You derive power from changing and challenging environments.
- Ambition and personal growth are your keywords.
- You are fluent in English and Dutch; French is a strong plus.

## What We Offer

- Cross-industry projects with a technology flavour.
- A personal coach and a tailored personal growth plan.
- The opportunity to spar with colleagues and your coach on difficult projects.
- Necessary training, courses, and certifications tailored to your personal interests.
- Konato team spirit: recurring team & knowledge sharing events, yearly teambuilding weekend, afterwork drinks, and more.
- Competitive salary package including a car and the usual fringe benefits.
