# AI Business Architect

![AI Business Architect](jobs/images/ai_business_architect.jpg)

At 9Yards, we help organisations design business and digital solutions that stand the test of time. As an AI Business Architect, you sit at the intersection of strategy, technology, and transformation — shaping how AI becomes a core driver of business value for our clients.

## What You'll Do

### AI strategy and architecture

- Define and maintain the enterprise AI strategy in alignment with business goals and digital transformation objectives.
- Design AI-enabled business models, workflows, and capabilities that enhance decision-making, automation, and customer experience.
- Establish architectural standards, governance frameworks, and best practices for AI solution deployment and lifecycle management.

### Opportunity identification and roadmapping

- Identify and evaluate opportunities for AI implementation across business functions, focusing on ROI, risk, and strategic fit.
- Collaborate with enterprise architects, data scientists, product managers, and business leaders to translate high-level AI use cases into executable roadmaps and solution architectures.

### Governance, compliance, and change

- Ensure ethical, secure, and compliant use of AI technologies in alignment with internal policies and external regulations.
- Monitor AI trends, technologies, and regulatory developments to inform long-term business and architecture strategies.
- Drive change management efforts to foster organisational readiness for AI transformation.

## Who You Are

- Strategic thinker with strong business acumen and a hands-on understanding of AI/ML technologies, platforms, and trends.
- Experienced in enterprise architecture and digital transformation programmes.
- Data-driven in your decision-making, with sharp analytical thinking.
- An excellent communicator and stakeholder manager — comfortable at boardroom level and in technical deep-dives alike.
- Knowledgeable in governance, risk, and compliance as they apply to AI applications.

## What We Offer

- Cross-industry projects with a technology flavour.
- A personal coach and a tailored personal growth plan.
- The opportunity to spar with colleagues and your coach on difficult projects.
- Necessary training, courses, and certifications tailored to your personal interests.
- Konato team spirit: recurring team & knowledge sharing events, yearly teambuilding weekend, afterwork drinks, and more.
- Competitive salary package including a car and the usual fringe benefits.
